The Beginning

Harold & Edna Ashworth, 1948

In the spring of 1948, Harold and Edna Ashworth paid $3,200 for 60 acres of Vermont hillside and six Jersey cows. Edna had a simple rule: if you wouldn't feed it to your children, don't sell it to anyone else's.

Today their granddaughter Clara runs the same land — now 400 acres of certified organic pasture — guided by the same principle, backed by modern soil science and regenerative grazing practices.

Our History

75 years in the making

1948

The farm is born

Harold and Edna Ashworth purchase 60 acres in Addison County, Vermont, with six Jersey cows and a dream.

1971

Second generation

Son Robert takes over, doubles the herd to 120, and builds the original cold-storage facility still used today.

1989

USDA Organic certified

Meadowpure becomes one of the first Vermont dairies to achieve USDA Organic certification.

2004

Third generation

Clara Ashworth-Hughes joins full-time after studying Agricultural Science at UVM, introducing regenerative grazing.

2018

Direct-to-consumer launch

Meadowpure launches online delivery, selling out the first 500 orders in 48 hours.

2024

B Corp Certified

After three years of assessment, Meadowpure earns B Corp certification.

Sustainability

Good for the cow. Good for the planet.

🌱

Regenerative Grazing

Our rotational grazing system allows pastures to fully recover, sequestering carbon and rebuilding soil health every year.

☀️

Solar-Powered Barns

Since 2020, 100% of our on-farm energy comes from solar panels installed across our barns and processing facilities.

♻️

Zero-Waste Packaging

Our bottles are 100% recyclable glass or rPET. We've eliminated single-use plastic from all packaging as of 2023.

🚛

Carbon-Neutral Delivery

We offset 100% of delivery emissions through verified reforestation projects.

💧

Water Stewardship

A closed-loop water recycling system has reduced our water consumption by 62% since 2019.

🐝

Biodiversity

We maintain 40 acres of wildflower meadow and host 18 managed bee colonies on our land.
